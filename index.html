WHY BIRDIES ARE DOWNGRADED
Birdie overs are not the best market on this setup. Aronimink has punished aggressive pins and putting. If a birdie line is inflated to 4.5, the under can be more logical than chasing over 3.5.
BEST MARKETS NOW
GIR, fairways hit, and pars are the best categories because they rely on repeatable tee-to-green control. Fairways are especially useful for accuracy players like Aaron Rai and Brian Harman. Pars are useful for leaders or careful chasers.
WEATHER READ
Saturday is mostly sunny and warmer. Drying greens and warmer air can help carry, but it also makes controlling approaches and leaving uphill putts more important.
PAIRING READ
Smalley/McNealy in the final group makes par overs stronger than birdie overs. Koepka/Rory is a focus group but not automatically a birdie group. Hideki/Gotterup creates a ball-striker versus hot-round contrast.
LINE RULE
Best plays are playable only at listed lines. GIR overs become much weaker if bumped from 11.5 to 12.5 or 12.5 to 13.5. Fairways over 8.5 is strong; fairways over 9.5 becomes a lean.
PRACTICE/WEEK READ
Practice week and course-tour content pointed to green slopes, severe pin areas, and Donald Ross-style angles. That is why ONYX is betting stable golf skills instead of assuming the course turns into a birdie fest.
HUMAN LOGIC
On Moving Day, leaders usually protect, chasers pick spots, and everyone knows one short-sided miss can turn into bogey. That is why pars and GIR make more sense than needing four or five birdies.
